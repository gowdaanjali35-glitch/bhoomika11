A patisserie that treats a classic with patience.

Layer & Luxe began with one recipe and one rule: nothing goes into the dish that we would not eat on its own. Our mascarpone is whipped by hand, our coffee is pulled fresh, and every tray rests for twelve hours before it is cut.

The result is a tiramisu that holds its shape, melts on the tongue and tastes like the last spoonful of a good dinner.

Four layers. Twelve hours. No shortcuts.
